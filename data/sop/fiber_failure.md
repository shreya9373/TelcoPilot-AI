# Fiber Backhaul Failure

## Overview
Fiber failures disconnect base stations from the core network, affecting multiple users.

## Symptoms
- Complete outage
- High packet loss
- Multiple towers offline

## Troubleshooting
1. Check optical signal.
2. Verify fiber continuity.
3. Inspect connectors.
4. Switch to backup path.

## Resolution
Repair damaged fiber or activate redundant routes.
