# Packet Loss Troubleshooting

## Overview
Packet loss occurs when network packets fail to reach their destination, leading to degraded application performance and unstable connectivity.

## Symptoms
- Voice distortion
- Video freezing
- Slow downloads
- High retransmissions

## Possible Causes
- Fiber damage
- Network congestion
- Faulty routers
- High CPU utilization
- Hardware failures

## Troubleshooting Procedure
1. Measure packet loss.
2. Verify fiber link status.
3. Inspect router interfaces.
4. Check switch health.
5. Restart failed devices.

## Resolution
Replace damaged links or reroute traffic if packet loss remains above acceptable thresholds.
